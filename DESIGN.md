# Design System: Bounty Operator

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Inspection Office"**

A finding goes through inspection before a triager ever sees it, and the site is that office's paperwork. Pages are forms printed in black on bond paper and canary stock: ruled fields, square corners, a part rule over every section, ledger tables with condensed caps for column heads. The review's decision comes back as a rubber stamp in ink, pressed on the draft at a slight tilt, and the one action the hunter takes prints as a solid box. Code is evidence, so it is pasted onto the form on carbon indigo, in a face that keeps every character distinct.

The density is that of a working form, not a brochure: captions in the margin, hairlines between rows, tabular figures, help printed under the control instead of hidden in a tooltip. Colour carries meaning before mood. Violet is stamp ink and means observed in the supplied files; ochre means still unproven; canary is stock, marking the page head, where you are and what you chose. Dark is the night shift of the same office: carbon indigo paper, pale print, lilac ink and canary for what you act on. The report slip stays paper, and code and the footer stay carbon, in both themes.

Motion is clerical and brief: colour changes in 120ms, panels rise 6px in 180ms, lists stagger 40ms apart. There is one authored moment, the stamp, which lands large and light and presses flat at its tilt. The confirmed anti-reference is the category's dark code-card hero: pages open on the field, and code appears as cited evidence inside a form.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Every page head prints on the field, full width under the header rule: canary by day, lifted carbon at night.
- Archivo condensed caps for margin captions, column heads, chips and stamps; the system face for running text; mono only for code and typed entries.
- Stamps, not pills: verdicts and severities print in ink outline and ink letters, and the large ones are tilted rubber stamps with their own inked impression.
- Square corners (2px, 3px on dialogs), 1px ink frames, hairlines inside, a 2px part rule over every section and under every table head.
- Paper lies flat; only the hero slip, a raised panel and a dialog cast a shadow.
- Two evidence inks, violet for observed and ochre for unproven, and a dashed line wherever something was not supplied.

## Colors

The palette is paperwork: black print on bond and white stock, canary stock for heads and marks, one violet stamp ink, ochre for the unproven, a set of severity inks, and carbon indigo for code and the night shift.

### Primary
- **Stamp Violet** (`accent`): the review's ink. Links, the focus ring by day, the observed chip, the Observed and resolved Counterargument labels of the finding card, the line numbers of a file reference, the pen stroke through a claimed severity, the Rewrite, then submit verdict, the running bar and the meter. Hover deepens it to **Deep Violet** (`accent-hover`).
- **Lilac Ink** (`accent-night`, hover `accent-hover-night`): the same ink on the night shift, lifted for contrast on carbon.

### Secondary
- **Canary** (`field`, `stock`): the stock. By day the page head prints on the canary field. In both themes canary fills the current nav item, the current step, the chosen segment, text selection and the Operator plan. Print on canary is Press Black, with **Canary Umber** (`on-field-muted`, `on-stock-muted`) for muted print.
- **Night Canary** (`stock-night`, `accent-solid-night`, `focus-night`, `field-rule-night`): at night canary becomes the action colour. It fills the primary box, draws the focus ring and prints the part rules on the night field.

### Tertiary
- **Carbon Indigo** (`code-bg`, deeper at night as `code-bg-night`): code prints on carbon in both themes, on the paper slip too, with **Carbon Print** (`code-text`), **Carbon Muted** (`code-muted`) for line numbers and context, and **Carbon Rule** (`code-line`). An observed line tints with **Code Violet** (`code-hl`), an unproven line with **Code Ochre** (`code-flag`). Only print turns code light.
- **Night Shift** (`bg-night`, `surface-night`, `surface-2-night`, `field-night`): the dark theme's paper. A carbon indigo page, a sheet lifted one step, an office-use shade lifted a step further, and the lifted carbon the page head prints on at night. The site footer prints on this stock in both themes.

### Neutral
- **Bond Paper** (`bg`): the page.
- **White Stock** (`surface`): cards, controls, the slip, table and sheet frames.
- **Office-Use Shade** (`surface-2`): the shaded box a form keeps for office use. Bars, form heads, table heads, the slip's verdict box, the inset card, skeletons.
- **Press Black** (`text`, `rule`, `on-field`, `on-stock`, `accent-solid`): print, printed rules and frames, and by day the solid fill of the action you take, whose hover turns Stamp Violet (`accent-solid-hover`).
- **Muted Ink** (`muted`): captions, ledes, secondary print.
- **Hairline** (`line`), **Strong Hairline** (`line-strong`) and **Control Line** (`line-control`, 3:1 on every surface): row dividers; the boxes of references and hashes and the neutral margin rule; the borders of form controls.
- The night counterparts carry the `-night` suffix: **Pale Print** (`text-night`), **Night Muted** (`muted-night`), **Night Rule** (`rule-night`) and the night hairlines.

### Severity and State
Stamp inks, each tuned per theme (the `-night` keys lift them for carbon):
- **Critical Red** (`sev-crit`, also `danger` and the Drop verdict), **High Orange** (`sev-high`, also Fix before deploy), **Medium Ochre** (`sev-med`), **Low Green** (`sev-low`, also `ok`, Submit and No blocking issues), **Info Blue** (`sev-info`, the info notice), **Unrated Grey** (`sev-none`, Hold: duplicate).
- **Ochre** (`gap`, with its tint `gap-bg`): unproven. Open objections, evidence gaps, Prove first, the needs-test and open statuses, the warning notice.

### Named Rules
**The Two Inks Rule.** Violet means observed in the supplied files, or an objection resolved; ochre means unproven: an open objection, a missing artifact, a test not run. No other colour carries evidence, and neither is spent on decoration.

**The Solid Action Rule.** The action you take is the only solid box: Press Black by day, canary at night. Every other button is a ruled box that fills with ink under the pointer.

**The Canary Is Stock Rule.** Canary fills; it never prints as letters or lines on paper. It is the field, the current nav item, the current step, the chosen option, a selection, the Operator plan. Only on carbon does it print: the footer's column titles and link hovers, and on the night shift the action box, the focus ring and the field's part rules. Anything set on canary prints in day ink in both themes.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (with Archivo fallback, then Arial Narrow, then system-ui)
**Body Font:** the system face (system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue)
**Label/Mono Font:** Archivo condensed caps for printed labels; ui-monospace (SF Mono, Cascadia Mono before Cascadia Code, Menlo, Consolas) for code

**Character:** One variable grotesque does all the printing. Archivo's width axis runs from the condensed black of a rubber stamp to the semi-condensed bold of a form's title line, so rank is set by width and weight instead of by a second face. Running text stays in the system face so it renders before the font arrives. Archivo is self-hosted as one variable file (weight 100 to 900, width 62% to 125%, Latin), preloaded on every page and licensed under the OFL. Until it arrives, headings print in "Archivo fallback": the machine's own Arial Narrow Bold declared as seven faces by width band, each with a `size-adjust` measured against Archivo, so a line takes close to the same room before and after the swap. No file is fetched for it, and heading measures are set in `em`, not `ch`, so the box does not change with the face. The match is close, not exact, so a line whose count must never change is fixed in the layout: on a phone a breadcrumb prints its last two crumbs, which fit one line in either face.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (860, 44px to 88px, line-height 0.93, width 72%): the home hero headline only, on the field, about 11 characters to the line.
- **Page head** (860, 36px to 60px, 0.98, width 76%; method and landing pages run to 64px): the h1 of every page head printed on the field.
- **Headline** (820, 32px to 52px, 1.02, width 88%, -0.012em): a plain h1 off the field.
- **Section** (800, 26px to 38px, 1.06, width 88%, -0.01em): the h2 under a part rule.
- **Title** (760, 22px, 1.18, width 88%): h3, the finding title (780, at most 56 characters wide), step titles.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.6, system face): running text. Prose runs at 1.7 with a 70ch measure.
- **Lede** (400, 18px, 1.55, Muted Ink): the paragraph under a page or section title, at most 60ch.
- **Body small** (400, 14px, 1.5): controls, tables, ledgers, notices; finding rows open to 1.65.
- **Label** (680, 12px, 0.07em, uppercase, width 85%): every printed caption. Margin labels, column heads, counters, ledger keys, cell labels, a hash's algorithm. Row labels and form heads take 720; footer titles take 720 at 0.08em.
- **Field caption** (700, 14px, width 90%, sentence case): the caption printed above a form control. Its hint sits beside it in the system face at 13px.
- **Button** (700, 15px, width 90%, 0.01em): buttons, tabs, stepper and segmented options, and nav links (650, the current one 760).
- **Chip** (780, 12px, 0.08em, uppercase, width 78%): severity, verdict, status and tone chips.
- **Stamp** (860, 18px, 0.1em, uppercase, width 68%): the rubber-stamp verdict. The hand-stamped severity is 14px at width 72% and 0.08em.
- **Code** (mono, 13px, 1.7, ligatures off): code blocks. File references and hashes run at 12px and 500; inline code at 0.875em, never under 12px, on an 8% ink tint.

Width is set with `font-stretch` against the face's declared range. Figures that are counted or ranked (counts, scores, prices, step numbers) are tabular and condensed: counts at 28px and width 75%, the price at 64px and width 68%.

### Named Rules
**The Width Is Rank Rule.** The narrower and heavier the cut, the louder the line: stamps at 68%, the hero at 72%, page heads at 76%, chips at 78%, labels at 85%, headings at 88%, controls at 90%, and running text in the system face. There is no second display face.

**The Condensed Caps Rule.** Every caption printed in a margin, a column head or a chip is Archivo in condensed caps. Mono is never a label: it is for what was typed or computed, such as code, paths, file references, hashes, model ids and the dater.

## Layout

The page is a stack of forms in one centred column. The wrap holds 75rem of content inside a fluid gutter of 16px to 44px; long-form pages narrow it to 46rem and wide views open it to 86rem. Sections pad 48px top and bottom, 72px from 768px; tight sections 32px, then 48px. Spacing steps on a 4px base (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 72); stacks default to 16px, rows inside forms pad 16px, cards pad 20px and panels 24px.

Every page opens on the field: the page head prints in a band the full width of the window, canary by day and lifted carbon at night, starting directly under the header rule while its content keeps to the wrap. The section holding it gives up its top padding. The band is painted outside the box, so it adds no sideways scroll.

On the home page the first viewport is that field. From 960px it holds two columns (1.05fr of text and 0.95fr of slip, 72px apart): the condensed headline, the lede, the solid Review my report box and the ruled View example box, then a note and two links. The tilted paper slip stands on the right, and the proof line sits on the field's foot under a 1px rule. On a phone the order is headline, lede, actions, slip, note, links, so the stamped verdict lands in the first screen. Up to 480px the price line ("Free: 1 review a day on your own model key.") and the note print directly under the actions, ahead of the slip; above 480px the price line is not printed.

Forms are ledgers. The finding card and the workbench sheet print a 9.75rem caption margin, one 1px rule and the content to its right, and under 704px the caption moves above its content. Key/value ledgers give the key 30% of the row (at least 6.5rem). Grids of cells are ruled by 1px gaps over the hairline ground inside a 1px frame, like the rack of verdict stamps and the benchmark facts.

Equal grids run one column, then two from 640px, then three or four from 960px; auto-fit grids keep columns at least 17.5rem wide. Split panes stand side by side from 960px (with a 15rem aside variant); a tool's input and result split from 1024px, the input staying in view. The header keeps the brand at left and the nav, theme toggle and account control at right. Under 928px the nav becomes a second row that scrolls sideways with no link hidden, and under 360px the wordmark leaves and the mark stays. Tables scroll sideways inside a focusable frame; stack tables read as one block per row under 704px (wide ones under 960px). On coarse pointers every small control grows to 44px.

Print is paper whatever the theme: light stock, deeper inks, flat cards and panels, no header, footer or buttons, code wrapped, every accordion answer printed and every external prose link spelled out.

### Named Rules
**The Part Rule.** A 2px printed rule opens every section, heads every ledger and table, and underlines the site header, the dialog head and the slip's head; a 1px hairline divides inside. Nothing sits above a heading but that rule: no eyebrow, no kicker, and a breadcrumb prints only from three levels deep.

**The Field Opens The Page Rule.** Every page head prints on the field, full width, directly under the header rule. A page never opens on a bare paper column or a dark code card.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Paper lies on the desk, and depth comes from stock and tone instead of light: the office-use shade for boxes kept for office use, the canary field for heads, carbon for code and the footer. Cards, rows, tables and sheets cast nothing at rest (`--shadow-card` is none). A shadow appears only when a loose sheet is laid on the form. Night shadows are deeper black, and on paper the cards, findings and panels print flat. Nothing lifts on hover; a pressed button sinks 1px.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Loose panel** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 1px #16161b14, 0 10px 24px -14px #16161b47`; night `0 1px 1px #0000004d, 0 12px 28px -14px #000000a6`): a tool panel, the at-a-glance box, the one-time token box, a raised card or finding.
- **Loose sheet** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px #16161b1f, 0 18px 36px -16px #16161b70`; night `0 1px 2px #00000066, 0 22px 44px -16px #000000b3`): the hero's report slip and a glowing finding card.
- **Pop** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 4px #16161b1f, 0 28px 60px -20px #16161b66`; night `0 2px 6px #00000059, 0 28px 64px -20px #000000cc`): dialogs, over a backdrop of Press Black at 55% (night: near-black at 78%).

### Named Rules
**The Loose Sheet Rule.** Paper lies flat. A shadow means a sheet laid over the form: the hero slip over its pink carbon copy, a raised tool panel, a dialog. It is never hover feedback, and never on a resting card.

## Shapes

Forms are square. Chips, controls, cards, tables and sheets turn their corners at 2px; dialogs, example frames and the call-to-action band at 3px. The rubber stamp is the only soft corner: 0.28em on the large verdict and 0.2em on the hand-stamped severity, set in em so one inked impression fits the stamp at every size. Circles appear only as marks: the status dot, tally dots, the radio mark of a pick and the busy spinner. List bullets are small squares (6 to 7px, 1px corners), tick boxes are 20px squares, and an evidence gap is an unticked dashed box.

Frames are 1px of Press Black around cards, tables, sheets, the slip and dialogs. Buttons, chips and tick boxes take 1.5px. The dossier carries a 4px head rule, and the large stamp a double rule (a 0.15em border with a 0.06em outline inset inside it). Only the slip and the stamps sit off square: the slip at -1.25° (-0.75° on a phone) over its carbon copy at +1.75°, the verdict stamp at -2° (-4° on the slip), the severity stamp at -3°.

**The Dashed Means Blank Rule.** A dashed line is a field left blank or a sheet that is not real: an unrated severity, Hold: duplicate, unsupplied code, an evidence gap, the frame around invented output. It is never decoration.

## Components

Markup, helper signatures and every variant are in `web/site/COMPONENTS.md`, and a dev build renders them all at `/_kit`. This section records how each one looks and behaves.

### Buttons
The boxes at the foot of a form: square, ruled, set in Archivo.
- **Shape:** square (2px), a 1.5px border, 44px minimum height; small is 36px and grows to 44px on touch, large is 52px.
- **Primary:** the solid action box, Press Black with white letters by day and canary with black letters at night, 18px side padding. Hover turns it Stamp Violet by day and a brighter canary at night.
- **Secondary:** a transparent box with a 1.5px Press Black rule. Under the pointer it fills with ink and its letters turn paper. On the field its rule prints in the field's ink.
- **Quiet / Danger:** quiet has no box and takes an 8% ink tint on hover; danger is a Critical Red outline that fills red on hover.
- **States:** colour and border change in 120ms; pressed sinks 1px; disabled sits at 50% opacity; a running button shows a spinner and keeps its label. Focus is a 2px ring 2px outside, violet by day and canary at night.
- **Links:** Stamp Violet with a half-strength 1px underline; on hover the ink deepens and the underline turns full strength and 2px.

### Chips
Stamps, not pills.
- **Style:** a 1.5px outline in the chip's ink, ink letters in condensed caps, and a trace of the same ink as fill (6% by day, 10% at night), 2px corners, 24px tall.
- **Variants:** severity (critical to info; unrated is dashed with no fill), verdict (seven decisions, each in its ink; Hold: duplicate dashed), status (a round dot leads the label and pulses while running; unsupplied and example are dashed), and tone (observed violet, unproven ochre, ok, danger, neutral).
- **Typed entries:** a file reference and a hash are mono at 12px in a 1px Strong Hairline box on White Stock. A reference keeps to one line; when space runs out its file part shortens and its line numbers, in violet, stay whole. A linked reference's box turns violet on hover.

### Stamps
The signature mark of the system: the verdict, stamped.
- **Rubber stamp:** the large verdict is a double rule (a 0.15em border with a 0.06em outline inset 0.36em) around condensed black caps (stamp typography), tilted -2°, or -4° on the slip, where it multiplies into the paper. The hand-stamped severity is a single heavier rule at width 72%, tilted -3°.
- **Impression:** each verdict and severity shows its ink through its own inked impression (`/stamps/verdict-<id>.webp`, `/stamps/sev-<id>.webp`) used as a CSS mask, so the ink is the chip's colour and day and night share one file. The words stay in the markup for search, selection and screen readers. A verdict or severity without an impression stays a crisp chip.
- **How impressions are made:** `scripts/build-stamps.mjs` renders the live stamp box crisp in Chromium (black, flat, untilted, 24px at 2x), then presses it with real ink from scans of real 1980s rubber-stamp imprints (CC0, `scripts/ink/SOURCES.md`). The voids, mottle and fibre are real solid ink quilted over the box from `scripts/ink/patches.png`, each patch matched to the ink it overlaps and joined along the seam of least difference; the pressure across the face is that of one real framed impression from `scripts/ink/pressure.png`, so strokes thin where it was pressed lightly; the edge keeps the soft spread measured on the scans and moves with the ink. No noise function is involved. Each stamp has its own seed and its own impression's pressure, and the same seed always gives the same file. `scripts/extract-ink.py` remakes the ink files from the two scans. The script also draws `strike.svg`, the tapered pen stroke that strikes a claimed severity. Re-run it when a label, the stamp geometry or the font changes; a test checks every verdict and severity has its file and the ink's provenance.
- **Landing:** a stamp lands large and light (from 155% and transparent), overshoots to 96% just past halfway and presses flat at its tilt in 420ms on the out-ease. A verdict at the head of an arriving result lands the same way. On the home slip the pen strike draws across in 260ms (starting at 380ms), Medium lands at 660ms and the verdict lands over 480ms at 1000ms. With reduced motion the stamps are simply there.

### Cards / Containers
A ruled box on the form.
- **Corner Style:** square (2px).
- **Background:** White Stock; the inset variant sits on the Office-Use Shade inside a Strong Hairline frame; the accent variant doubles a violet frame.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest (see Elevation & Depth); the raised variant is a loose panel.
- **Border:** a 1px Press Black frame, with hairlines between head, body and foot.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px by 20px in the head, 20px in the body, 12px by 20px in the foot. Cards never nest.
- **Dossier:** the slip at the head of a result. The verdict stamp, the headline (Archivo, 19px, 680, width 92%) and the counts (28px condensed numerals in severity ink, zero in Muted Ink), under a 4px head rule.
- **Tool panel:** the frame around a free tool. It opens with a form head on the Office-Use Shade over a 2px rule, printing the form's number in a ruled box, its name and a tag in condensed caps.

### Inputs / Fields

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour, size, radius and duration from the tokens in `web/public/css/base.css` section 1. A new state colour goes into all three theme blocks (day, night by preference, night by choice), and a test keeps the two night blocks identical.
- **Do** open every page on the field and every section with the 2px part rule.
- **Do** print margin captions, column heads, chips and stamps in Archivo condensed caps (12px, width 85%, 680 to 720, 0.07em), and keep mono for code, paths, references, hashes, model ids and typed entries.
- **Do** carry a row's standing in its caption's ink, violet for observed and ochre for unproven, beside one neutral 1px margin rule that turns dashed where something was not supplied.
- **Do** print verdicts and severities as stamps (an ink outline, ink letters, a trace of fill), give a large verdict its inked impression, and re-run `scripts/build-stamps.mjs` when a label, the stamp geometry or the font changes.
- **Do** keep code on carbon indigo in both themes, with observed lines in violet and unproven lines in ochre.
- **Do** put invented output in a dashed frame labelled Example.
- **Do** keep every touch target at 44px, except a file reference inside a sentence, which keeps the height of its line (25px); keep every text at 12px or more, and form controls at 16px under 768px.
- **Do** honour reduced motion: transitions and animations stop, the running bar becomes a solid line and skeletons go flat.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put anything above a heading: no eyebrow, no kicker, no two-level breadcrumb.
- **Don't** mark status with a coloured stripe on the edge of a card, ledger row, tab, list item or fact panel; the ink of the caption carries it.
- **Don't** set captions, column heads or buttons in mono, or add a second display face; Archivo is the one webfont, and it is self-hosted.
- **Don't** round a corner past 3px or draw a pill; the stamp's em corner is the only soft one (the brand mark keeps its own tile), and circles are kept for marks: dots, radio marks and the spinner.
- **Don't** shadow a resting card, row or table, or lift anything on hover; shadows belong to the slip, raised panels and dialogs.
- **Don't** fake ink with a shared, repeating noise tile or a texture overlay; each stamp carries its own impression.
- **Don't** dress a tool or code panel as a terminal window; a tool panel opens with a form head (the form's number, its name and a tag in condensed caps on the Office-Use Shade over a 2px rule).
- **Don't** lead a page with a dark code card; the page head prints on the field and code appears as cited evidence inside a form.
- **Don't** add a hex value outside the token blocks, an inline style, or a font or image from another origin; the CSP blocks them and the build fails.
- **Don't** nest cards, or hide help in a tooltip.
